## v1 Requirements (July 13 Milestone)

- [ ] **EVAL-01**: Automated AI scoring across 5+ competencies (Technical, Behavioral, etc.).

- [ ] **EVAL-03**: Verified Score™ generation and badge display.

- [ ] **SEARCH-02**: AI-powered search results view highlighting candidate "Fit Reasoning".
- [ ] **LEAD-01**: Talent leaderboard with ranking based on verified performance metrics.
