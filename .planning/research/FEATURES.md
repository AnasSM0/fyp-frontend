# FEATURES.md — Premium AI Assessment UX Patterns

> Status: Research Recommended for Demo Impact

---

## ✅ Table Stakes (Must Have)
- **Competency-Based Scoring**: Reports must be structured around specific, job-validated competencies (e.g., "System Design", "Communication") rather than generic scores.
- **Evidence-Backed Insights**: Include transcript snippets or specific behavioral observations to justify AI ratings.
- **System Readiness Checks**: Auto-detect camera, mic, and connection stability before the interview begins (prevents demo crashes).
- **Text-Driven Evaluation**: Focus on semantic content of answers rather than unreliable/biased facial sentiment analysis.

## ✨ Differentiators (WOW Factors)
- **"Modern Elite" Aesthetics**: High-density layouts, 1px muted borders, and monochromatic bases inspired by Linear and Vercel.
- **Cinematic Onboarding**: Use Framer Motion for a 3D-feeling "processing" sequence when generating the interview blueprint.
- **Calibrated Trust**: Visualize AI confidence levels (High/Medium/Low) using soft color signals instead of confusing raw probability numbers.
- **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) UX**: Optional "AI Reasoning" tooltips that show the recruiter *why* the AI flagged a specific strength.

## 🚫 Anti-Features (Avoid)
- **Low-Density Dashboards**: Generic templates with excessive whitespace that look like "AI generated" fluff.
- **Intrusive Proctoring**: Avoid overwhelming "Eye tracking" or "Face lock" UI that creates candidate anxiety.
- **Raw Probability Dumps**: Never show a score without context or reasoning.
